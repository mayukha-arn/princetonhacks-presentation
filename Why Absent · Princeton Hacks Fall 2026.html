Where are all the kids, and how much is this costing us? We find out why students miss school, trace it to a root cause, and price the fix.

The societal need

Schools know a child was absent. They almost never know why.

19%

chronically absent in California, 2024–25

About 1.1 million students. Down from a 30% peak, still well above the 12% pre-pandemic baseline.

$630–1,800

lost per chronically absent student, per year

Attendance-funded states lose money on every missed day. Vendor estimate, unverified.

2.4–3.6pts

drop in chronic absence from adaptive texting

National IES trial, ~26,000 elementary students, at $6.90–$8.53 per student per year.

0

tools that link barriers across families

Incumbents automate messaging. None surfaces shared causes like one bus route or one classroom.

What Why Absent adds

A structured barrier for every absence, clusters that reveal a bus, classroom or household cause, and a school action priced in dollars per week. Safety-critical replies skip automation and go straight to a counselor.

Families are partners, not violators. Outreach asks what is getting in the way and offers help. Risk of harm sets priority; dollars only justify the program.
Sources: California chronic absenteeism and economics figures from the Why Absent PRD (daily rate $35–$100 × 18 days); IES adaptive-texting trial cited in the PRD.

Architecture & tech stack

One API, swappable intelligence, a safety gate up front.

Frontend

Staff dashboard

  • React 18 + TypeScript
  • Vite + React Router
  • Role views: clerk, counselor, business manager
  • Revenue dashboard with editable rate
Backend

FastAPI core

  • Python, FastAPI, Pydantic v2
  • CSV import from any SIS export
  • Audit trail for every message and approval
  • TigerData Postgres time series planned
AI & graph

Reasons to clusters

  • Barrier classifier with evidence quote stub → Gemini
  • Whisper voice notes, any language planned
  • networkx multi-layer graph SocioPatterns + synthetic
  • Plans grounded in the pacing guide
Messaging & trust

Photon 2.0 iMessage

  • Two-way family channel, polled every 2s
  • SMS fallback so Android families are included planned
  • Every reply is untrusted data, strict JSON out
  • Synthetic data only, zero real PII
